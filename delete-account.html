Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: August 29, 2026

You can permanently delete your DueGold account and all data associated with it at any time, either directly in the app or by requesting it here. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (instant)

Recommended

Steps

  1. Open DueGold and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm when prompted.

Your account and all data are deleted immediately — no waiting period.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed or can't sign in, email us from the address associated with your account and we'll delete it for you.

What to include

  • Send from the same email address you used to sign in to DueGold.
  • Subject line: "Delete my DueGold account".

We process email requests within 7 days and will confirm once your data has been deleted.

Deleting individual items, without deleting your account

You don't have to delete your whole account to remove specific data. Inside the app, you can delete any of the following on their own, at any time:

What you can delete individually

  • A single loan — including its payment history and jewel photos — from the loan's detail screen.
  • A single payment record — from that loan's payment history, without affecting the loan itself.
  • A jewel photo — by editing the loan and removing the photo, without deleting the loan or any other jewel details.

Each of these takes effect immediately and permanently — your account and everything else you haven't chosen to delete stays exactly as it was.

This action cannot be undone. Once deleted, your loans, payment history, and jewel photos cannot be recovered — including by us. If you just want to stop using the app without losing your data, simply sign out instead of deleting your account.
