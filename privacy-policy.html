Gold Loan Manager

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

DueGold: Gold Loan Tracker ("DueGold", "the app", "we"), developed by Sathyajithray Namadevan, is a personal finance app that helps you track gold loans you've taken from banks or lenders — amounts, repayment progress, due dates, and details about the jewellery pledged as collateral. This page explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and how you can access or delete it.

DueGold does not lend money, broker loans, or connect you with any lender. It is purely a personal record-keeping tool for loans you already hold elsewhere.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your email address, via Google Sign-In or email/password sign-in. If Google provides a display name or profile picture, the app may show it in your profile; your name can also label an account you share.
  • Loan information you enter — loan names, bank/lender names, amounts, interest rates, pledge and due dates, repayment history, and any notes you add.
  • Jewellery details — descriptions, weights, quantities, and photos of pledged items, if you choose to add them. The photos you select or take are uploaded to private storage for your account.
  • Sharing information — if you invite someone to a shared account, we store their sign-in email and your account email and display name. Invited users can view and change the loans, payments, jewellery details, and photos in that account until access is removed.
  • Purchase status — whether you've bought the app's one-time "unlimited loans" unlock, together with a store purchase token used to verify and restore it. Android payment is handled by Google Play; DueGold does not receive your payment card details.
  • On-device information — unfinished loan drafts, settings, and a small technical log may be stored on your device. The log is not sent to us automatically; you can choose to share it with support.

How we use your information

Everything you enter is used only to provide the app's own features: tracking your loans, calculating progress and interest, sending due-date reminders, sharing an account when you invite someone, and verifying your purchase/unlock status. Reminders are scheduled on your device.

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for any purpose other than running the app. There are no ads and no analytics/tracking SDKs in this app.

Third-party services we rely on

  • Supabase — our backend provider, which hosts the database, authentication, and private file storage your data lives in. Supabase processes this data on our behalf under their own security practices.
  • Google Sign-In — used to authenticate you; Google may also provide your display name and profile picture.
  • Google Play Billing — processes the one-time in-app purchase on Android. Payment card details never pass through DueGold's own systems.

How your data is protected

Database-level row security limits loan, payment, and jewel access to you and people you explicitly invite to your shared account. Jewel photos are kept in a private storage bucket and accessed through time-limited links. Removing a collaborator stops new access, but a photo link already issued to them may remain usable until it expires, for up to seven days.

Optional device access

You can select multiple individual photos through your device's photo picker without giving DueGold access to your whole photo library. Camera access is requested only if you choose to take a photo. Notification permission is requested for loan reminders. You can disable reminders in the app or device settings. The app does not request contacts or device location.

Your rights, and how to delete your data

You can access, correct, or delete everything you've entered at any time:

In the app: open the Profile tab → scroll to the bottom → Delete Account. This removes your account, its records, and associated jewel photos from the active service. It does not delete another person's account if you were a collaborator.

Without the app: see our Account & Data Deletion page for how to request deletion by email instead.

Deleting your account is irreversible in the app. Copies you previously exported or shared are outside the app and must be removed where you saved them.

Data retention

We keep your account data while your account exists. When you delete the account, we remove its records and associated jewel photos from the active database and storage. Service-provider backups may be retained temporarily under the provider's backup schedule. Information you exported or shared, including copies on another person's device, is not deleted by this action.

Children's privacy

DueGold is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally as the app changes. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.
